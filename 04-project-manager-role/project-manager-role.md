# Project Manager Role

## 1. Overview

A Project Manager is responsible for planning, coordinating, monitoring, and controlling a project to achieve its objectives.

For the Food Delivery Mobile Application, the Project Manager coordinates the different teams and stakeholders involved in delivering the application.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities

The major responsibilities of the Project Manager include:

- Scope management
- Schedule management
- Cost management
- Quality management
- Risk management
- Resource management
- Communication management
- Stakeholder management
- Change management
- Issue management
- Decision making
- Project reporting

---

## 3. Scope Management

The Project Manager ensures that the team understands what is included and excluded from the project.

For example, if a stakeholder requests a new grocery delivery feature, the Project Manager evaluates its impact on:

- Scope
- Schedule
- Cost
- Resources
- Risks

---

## 4. Schedule Management

The Project Manager creates and monitors the project schedule.

Example:

| Activity | Planned Duration |
|---|---:|
| Requirements | 2 weeks |
| UI/UX Design | 3 weeks |
| Development | 8 weeks |
| Testing | 4 weeks |
| Deployment | 1 week |

---

## 5. Cost Management

The Project Manager monitors project spending against the approved budget.

Example:

| Resource | Estimated Cost |
|---|---:|
| Developers | ₹4,00,000 |
| Designers | ₹1,00,000 |
| QA | ₹1,00,000 |
| Cloud | ₹50,000 |
| Software/Tools | ₹50,000 |
| Other | ₹1,00,000 |
| Total | ₹8,00,000 |

---

## 6. Quality Management

The Project Manager ensures that the project meets agreed quality requirements.

For the Food Delivery App, important quality expectations may include:

- Reliable login
- Successful payment processing
- Accurate order tracking
- Low application crash rate
- Secure customer information

---

## 7. Risk Management

The Project Manager identifies and manages potential future problems.

Example:

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payment integration delay | High | High | Start integration early and maintain a backup provider |

---

## 8. People and Resource Management

The Project Manager coordinates people and resources required to complete the project.

Key team members may include:

- Developers
- UI/UX Designers
- QA Engineers
- Business Analysts
- DevOps Engineers
- Product Manager

---

## 9. Communication Management

The Project Manager ensures that the right information reaches the right people at the right time.

Different stakeholders require different levels of information.

---

## 10. Stakeholder Management

The Project Manager identifies stakeholders, understands their expectations, communicates with them, and manages their involvement throughout the project.

Key stakeholders include:

- Project Sponsor
- Product Manager
- Development Team
- QA Team
- Restaurant Partners
- Delivery Partners
- Customers
- Payment Provider

---

## 11. Change Management

When a stakeholder requests a change, the Project Manager evaluates its impact before approval.

The evaluation may consider:

- Scope
- Cost
- Schedule
- Resources
- Quality
- Risks

---

## 12. Issue Management

An issue is a problem that has already occurred.

Example:

**Risk:** Payment provider may become unavailable.

**Issue:** Payment provider is currently unavailable.

The Project Manager tracks issues and coordinates corrective actions.

---

## 13. What the Project Manager Does Not Necessarily Do

The Project Manager does not necessarily perform all technical work.

For example:

| Role | Main Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Developer | Builds software |
| Designer | Designs user interface |
| QA Engineer | Tests software |
| DevOps Engineer | Manages infrastructure |
| Business Analyst | Analyzes requirements |
| Project Manager | Coordinates the overall project |

---

## 14. Conclusion

The Project Manager's main responsibility is to coordinate the project and help ensure that the agreed objectives are achieved while managing scope, schedule, cost, quality, risks, resources, stakeholders, communication, issues, and changes.
