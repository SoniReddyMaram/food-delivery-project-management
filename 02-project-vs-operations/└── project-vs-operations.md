# Project vs Operations

## 1. Introduction

Projects and operations are both important to an organization, but they have different purposes.

A project is temporary work performed to create a unique product, service, or result.

Operations are ongoing activities performed to keep the organization or business functioning.

---

## 2. Key Difference

The simplest way to remember the difference is:

> **Projects create change. Operations keep the business running.**

---

## 3. Project Example — Food Delivery App

The development of a new Food Delivery Mobile Application is a project.

The project includes:

* Gathering requirements
* Designing the application
* Developing the application
* Testing the application
* Integrating payments
* Deploying the application
* Launching the application

The project has a defined objective and will eventually be completed.

---

## 4. Operations Example — Food Delivery Business

After the application is launched, ongoing activities become operations.

Examples include:

* Processing customer orders
* Supporting customers
* Processing payments
* Managing restaurants
* Managing delivery partners
* Monitoring servers
* Handling refunds
* Maintaining the application

These activities continue as part of normal business operations.

---

## 5. Comparison

| Project                       | Operations                             |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Temporary                     | Ongoing                                |
| Has a defined beginning       | Continues indefinitely                 |
| Has a defined end             | Usually has no defined end             |
| Creates something new         | Maintains existing business activities |
| Has a specific objective      | Has ongoing objectives                 |
| Example: Build an application | Example: Operate the application       |

---

## 6. Project-to-Operations Transition

A project can create a new product, system, or process that later becomes part of normal operations.

Example:

```text
Business Need
      ↓
Project
      ↓
Develop Food Delivery App
      ↓
Testing
      ↓
Launch
      ↓
Operations
      ↓
Process Orders
Maintain App
Support Customers
Monitor Systems
```

---

## 7. Additional Examples

### Project

Build a new company website.

### Operations

Maintain and update the existing website every day.

### Project

Open a new company office.

### Operations

Run the office every day.

### Project

Develop a new banking application.

### Operations

Process banking transactions every day.

---

## 8. Conclusion

Projects and operations work together.

Projects introduce change and create new products, services, or processes.

Operations use and maintain those products, services, or processes to keep the business running.
