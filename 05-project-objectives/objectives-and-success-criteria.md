# Project Objectives and Success Criteria

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of the Food Delivery Mobile Application project is to design, develop, test, and launch a minimum viable food delivery application within 6 months and an approved budget of ₹10 lakh.

The application will allow customers to:

- Browse restaurants
- Browse menus
- Place food orders
- Make online payments
- Track deliveries

---

## 2. Business Objective

The business objective is to increase online food orders and revenue by providing customers with a convenient digital food-ordering platform.

---

## 3. SMART Objective

The project will design, develop, test, and launch a minimum viable food delivery application within 6 months and a ₹10 lakh budget, enabling customers to browse restaurants, place orders, make online payments, and track deliveries.

### SMART Analysis

| SMART Element | Application to Project |
|---|---|
| Specific | Build a food delivery application with defined core features |
| Measurable | 6-month timeline, ₹10 lakh budget, and defined functionality |
| Achievable | Objective should be aligned with available resources |
| Relevant | Supports the business goal of increasing online orders |
| Time-bound | Application must be launched within 6 months |

---

## 4. Project Success Criteria

| Area | Success Criterion |
|---|---|
| Schedule | Application launched within 6 months |
| Budget | Total project cost does not exceed ₹10 lakh |
| Scope | All approved MVP features are delivered |
| Quality | No critical defects remain at launch |
| Functionality | Customers can browse, order, pay, and track deliveries |
| Performance | Application meets agreed performance requirements |
| Business | Achieve the agreed first-month order target |

---

## 5. Project KPIs

Project performance can be monitored using:

- Project completion percentage
- Schedule variance
- Cost variance
- Number of open risks
- Number of open issues
- Number of critical defects
- Requirements completion percentage

---

## 6. Project KPIs vs Business KPIs

### Project KPIs

Measure how effectively the project is being delivered.

Examples:

- Schedule variance
- Budget variance
- Defect count
- Requirements completion

### Business KPIs

Measure the value created by the resulting product.

Examples:

- Number of orders
- Revenue
- Customer satisfaction
- Customer retention
- User adoption

---

## 7. Objective vs Deliverable vs Success Criteria

| Concept | Meaning | Food Delivery Example |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | What we want to achieve | Launch a food delivery application |
| Deliverable | What we produce | Customer mobile application |
| Success Criteria | How we know we succeeded | Application launches within 6 months and meets agreed requirements |

---

## 8. Conclusion

Clear project objectives provide direction to the project team.

Success criteria provide measurable conditions for determining whether the project has achieved its objectives.

Using SMART objectives helps make project goals specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.
