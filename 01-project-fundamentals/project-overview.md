# Project Overview

## 1. Project Name

**Food Delivery Mobile Application**

---

## 2. Project Description

The project involves designing, developing, testing, and launching a mobile food delivery application.

The application will allow customers to discover restaurants, browse menus, order food, make online payments, and track their deliveries.

---

## 3. Business Objective

The business wants to increase online food orders and revenue by providing customers with a convenient digital ordering platform.

---

## 4. Project Objective

The project objective is to design, develop, test, and launch a functional food delivery application within the approved scope, timeline, budget, and quality requirements.

---

## 5. Major Deliverables

1. Customer mobile application
2. Restaurant management system
3. Backend/API
4. Database
5. Online payment integration
6. Delivery tracking system
7. Admin dashboard
8. Testing documentation
9. Production deployment

## 7. Initial Risks

### Risk 1 — Development Delay

Development tasks may take longer than estimated, causing the launch date to move.

### Risk 2 — Changing Requirements

Stakeholders may request additional features during development, increasing scope, cost, and schedule.

### Risk 3 — Payment Integration Failure

Problems with the payment provider or integration could prevent customers from completing orders.

### Risk 4 — Security Problems

Security vulnerabilities could expose customer or payment-related information.

### Risk 5 — Budget Overrun

Unexpected development or infrastructure costs could cause the project to exceed its budget.

---

## 8. Initial Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

* The application is successfully launched.
* All agreed MVP requirements are completed.
* The project remains within the approved budget.
* The project meets the agreed schedule.
* Critical defects are resolved before launch.
* Customers can successfully place and pay for orders.
* Customers can track their deliveries.
