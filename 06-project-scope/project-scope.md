# Project Scope

## 1. Overview

Project scope defines the boundaries of the Food Delivery Mobile Application project.

It specifies what work, features, and deliverables are included in the project and what is excluded from the initial release.

---

## 2. Project Scope Statement

The project will deliver a minimum viable food delivery platform that enables customers to register, browse restaurants and menus, place food orders, make online payments, and track delivery status.

The platform will also provide restaurants with basic order and menu management capabilities and administrators with basic platform management functionality.

---

## 3. In-Scope Items

### Customer Features

- User registration
- User login
- Restaurant search
- Restaurant filtering
- Menu browsing
- Shopping cart
- Order placement
- Order history

### Payment

- Credit/debit card payments
- Digital payments
- Payment confirmation
- Failed payment handling

### Delivery

- Delivery status
- Basic order tracking
- Delivery notifications

### Restaurant Features

- Restaurant registration
- Menu management
- Order management

### Admin Features

- User management
- Restaurant management
- Order monitoring
- Basic reporting

---

## 4. Out-of-Scope Items

The following features are excluded from the initial release:

- Grocery delivery
- Hotel booking
- Flight booking
- Restaurant hardware/POS systems
- International delivery
- Cryptocurrency payments
- Advanced AI recommendations
- Drone delivery

---

## 5. Scope Boundaries

The project focuses on delivering a food ordering and delivery platform.

Features unrelated to food ordering and delivery are excluded unless formally approved through the project's change management process.

---

## 6. Scope Creep

Scope creep is the uncontrolled expansion of project scope without proper evaluation and approval.

Examples include:

- Adding grocery delivery without changing the project plan
- Adding hotel booking without additional resources
- Adding advanced AI features without evaluating cost and schedule
- Adding cryptocurrency payments without formal approval

---

## 7. Scope Change Process

A proposed scope change should follow a controlled process:

1. Submit change request
2. Understand the requested change
3. Analyze impact
4. Evaluate cost, schedule, resources, quality, and risk
5. Obtain stakeholder approval
6. Update project documentation
7. Implement the approved change

---

## 8. Scope Baseline

The approved scope represents the initial scope baseline.

Any proposed change should be evaluated against the approved baseline.

---

## 9. Acceptance Criteria

The customer ordering functionality will be considered complete when:

- Customers can select a restaurant.
- Customers can browse menu items.
- Customers can add items to the cart.
- Customers can place an order.
- Customers can complete payment.
- Customers receive order confirmation.

---

## 10. Conclusion

Clearly defining project scope helps the team understand what must be delivered and prevents uncontrolled expansion of project work.

Effective scope management requires clear boundaries, stakeholder agreement, change control, and regular validation of project deliverables.
